Statutory Mandate

Students' Grievance Redressal Mechanism

Established under UGC & AICTE guidelines to ensure a fair, transparent, and student-centric academic environment.

Confidentiality & Safety

Complaints can be lodged anonymously. The identity of the complainant is safeguarded strictly against any form of academic victimization.

Strict SLA Timelines

Every complaint is acknowledged within 24 hours and assigned directly to the responsible section with a mandatory closure timeline of 3 to 7 working days.

Multi-tier Escalation

Unresolved complaints automatically escalate from Department In-charge to Head of Department, and eventually to the Principal / Ombudsman.
