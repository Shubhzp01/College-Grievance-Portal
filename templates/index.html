How The Grievance Process Works

A streamlined 4-step transparent cycle from grievance lodgement to closed verification.

1
1. Register Complaint

Choose category, describe the issue, upload supporting evidence/photos, or submit anonymously.

2
2. Review & Assign

Admin reviews the issue, assigns it to the designated department/technician, and sets priority.

3
3. Track Progress

Track timeline milestones, inspect administrative action remarks, and observe step-by-step progress.

4
4. Resolution & Feedback

Once marked resolved, students verify the fix and submit a 1 to 5 star satisfaction rating.
